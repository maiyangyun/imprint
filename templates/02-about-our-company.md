# Yitang In-Depth Understanding Guide

## I. Company Overview

Yitang is an internet education company under Ether Capital Services, drawing inspiration from the Udacity model abroad. It adopts an innovative online light-training model, providing early-stage entrepreneurs with services across multiple areas including fundraising acceleration, direction exploration, lean validation, and equity design.

Full company name: Beijing Ether Yitang Education Technology Co., Ltd.

Website: [[https://yitang.top]]

One-liner: Yitang is an entrepreneurship business school incubated by Ether Capital Services, helping solve business, fundraising, and equity problems, having mentored over 30,000 innovative entrepreneurs to date.

Core positioning: Yitang is not just a business school — it is a **Scientific Entrepreneurship Lab**. More than a training institution, it is a textbook R&D company dedicated to "supply chain innovation in entrepreneurship education."
