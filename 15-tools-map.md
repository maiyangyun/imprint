## 🏗️ Part V: Common Questions & Capability Boundaries

### 1. Common Questions (Q&A)
- **Q**: Is it better to give AI more tools?
- **A**: **Absolutely not**. Too many tools severely consume Context (token burn) and cause AI "decision paralysis," even invoking incorrect parameters. **Recommendation: no more than 10 core tools per Agent**.
- **Q**: Why does AI tool invocation often fail?
- **A**: Check whether your `TOOLS.md` provides the correct aliases (e.g., `Camera: front-door`). AI depends on these **explicit names**.
